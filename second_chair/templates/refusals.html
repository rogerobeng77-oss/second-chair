The rules

What counts as advice

  • Anything of the form “should I”, “can I”, “is it safe to”.
  • Anything asking how much to take, or what happens if a dose is missed, skipped or doubled.
  • Anything asking whether a symptom is serious, normal, urgent or an emergency.

The same three rules run over the model's own recap before it is displayed, and a sentence that trips one is removed whole rather than edited. The count of removals is on the front page.
