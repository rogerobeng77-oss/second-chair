The honest version of rule three

The rule says discard segments with no expected speaker present, and the obvious way to do that is voice recognition. Bee cannot do voice recognition and neither can we. So this is scoped on the one boundary the API does give us, the conversation identifier. That is weaker, and it is written here rather than left for someone to discover.

The part that does not go away

A wearable in a clinic makes health information

45 CFR 164.508(a)(1) means a recording made in a consulting room on a consumer device sits outside the covered entity's control. Second Chair cannot solve that, and an app that claimed to would be lying. What it does instead is refuse to become the record:

  • the transcript has an expiry and the clock is on the front page;
  • any row can be overruled by what the clinic's own summary says, and the correction is what exports;
  • the export and the family summary both open and close by saying the clinic's document is the one that counts;
  • nothing is sent anywhere except the model call that writes the recap, and that can be switched off in settings with every page still working.
